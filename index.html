About Us

Roswell Robo Squad Robotics is a nonprofit organization located in Roswell, New Mexico. We provide free robotics and STEM education to students in kindergarten through 12th grade.

Why I Care About Robotics

As the founder and executive director, I enjoy helping students discover technology, engineering, programming, and teamwork.

Our Activities

What Students Learn

  • Robot design and construction
  • Computer programming
  • 3D printing and design
  • Engineering and problem-solving
  • Teamwork and leadership

Preparing for Competitions

  1. Design a robot.
  2. Build the robot.
  3. Program the robot.
  4. Test and improve the robot.
  5. Participate in competitions.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make robotics and STEM education accessible to all students, regardless of their financial circumstances.
